Acid composition: character before catalogue size

Research for Samir Ettali · Sottofondo · 6 September 2026

The direction supported by the references

Sottofondo should explore a more forceful rave sound: a recognizable acid foreground, a complementary rhythmic foundation, and deliberate changes in articulation, texture and tension. More supporting sounds can help, particularly sustained atmosphere, contrasting riffs and processed accents. Their value depends on what they contribute to the composition. The present evidence does not identify instrument count as the principal cause of the user's dissatisfaction.

This is a research recommendation, not a completed listening result. The scope is the confirmed Blade rave recording, the exact Colin McRae upload supplied by the user, relevant producer accounts, and the current local preview at commit c0187d7. Music generation must remain entirely local. The research does not reproduce either reference's melody or import its recording into the app.

1. What the two references actually establish

Blade: a selected hook and a distinctive processed voice

The confirmed recording is New Order's Confusion (Pump Panel Reconstruction Mix). Tim Taylor describes making it with Dan Zamani using a vocoded fragment of the original vocal, a TR-909 and a TB-303. He recalls spending three studio days developing the acid riff. This is substantially new instrumental material rather than the original 1983 arrangement with an added synthesizer. Tim Taylor interviewed by Mike Mannix, Iconic Underground, 4 February 2018.

A second creator account reports more than forty bassline candidates and a decisive experiment with an unidentified guitar-effect pedal. Its make and model remain unknown. Red Bull Music Academy describes the Blade cue as acid techno and distinguishes it from later tracks reusing the riff, including Operation Blade and Phatt Bass. The account supports searching jointly for a compelling phrase and a processed sound. Forty is historical context, not an algorithm parameter to copy. Gabriel Szatan, Red Bull Music Academy, 26 February 2019.

For this project, Blade therefore points toward an assertive, distorted foreground and a memorable refrain. Automatically giving every seed an uplifting chord progression and a bright arpeggio could steer the result away from that reference. This last conclusion is our interpretation, not a producer statement.

Colin McRae: analyze the supplied recording, keep its provenance bounded

YouTube's metadata identifies the supplied video as Colin McRae Rally 2.0 Soundtrack – Jonathan Colling Theme Original Version, uploaded by Rally Games Soundtracks. Jonathan Colling's own Octagon Music site confirms his work on the game series; the soundtrack's distributed catalogue credits the theme to him. These establish identity and attribution, not a studio equipment list. User-selected recording, Octagon Music, first-party profile, updated content dated 2024, Apple Music, theme listing.

The local file lasts 85.061 seconds. Two separate Gemini 3.8 Flash requests received only its audio, without title, artist or a requested genre. Both described a foreground sequenced synth, a steady electronic kick/bass foundation, crisp subdivisions, faint sustained background sound, and development through repetition, brightness and layer density. This is useful provisional evidence for combining a driving repeated figure with background depth.

The reports disagreed about exact entrances, tempo and genre labels. Consequently, this report does not publish an exact section transcription, key, chord progression, synthesizer model or definitive genre classification for Colin McRae. Agreement between two calls to the same model is not independent human verification. No original session breakdown by Colling was found in the consulted sources.

2. How the music acquires its character

The acid part includes articulation

A 303 phrase involves pitch, rests, gate, accent and slide. Roland documents accents affecting level and filter response, and uses contrasting staccato and sliding steps as part of pattern development. Its suggested workflow includes generating material, refining it and varying a recognizable pattern. Christian Moraga, Roland, 3 March 2025.

Robin Whittle's circuit documentation explains why independent notes approximate only part of that behavior. On the standard 303, accent changes envelope behavior; the sweep circuit retains charge, so adjacent accents interact. The internal sequencer joins slid notes into one envelope event while the pitch slews. A separate pitch sweep on every newly triggered note does not reproduce that articulation. The manual distinguishes these original behaviors from Devil Fish modifications. Robin Whittle, Devil Fish User Manual, 9 February 2019, printed pp. 4–5 and 32.

Richness can come from layers of the same musical idea

Armin van Buuren describes starting with an eight-bar theme, developing contrasting sections, deriving additional layers from existing material, and using extensive automation. Sound choice and complementary frequency ranges matter; he also warns against repeatedly using the same sounds. This is a first-person melodic-trance workflow from 2009, not a universal rule for 1990s acid. Mike Senior, Producing Trance, Sound On Sound, August 2009.

Josh Wink reports two distorted 303s, a 909 kick, sampled snares and background string chords for Higher State of Consciousness, with a performance recorded in one take. He cannot confidently recall which synth produced the pad. His account illustrates an interacting foreground and supporting atmosphere; it does not establish that every acid composition requires two 303s. FAZE interview, 29 January 2020.

There is more than one valid starting process

Papa Nugs describes writing drums, then bass, then melodic lines in an eight-bar loop, followed by arranging different combinations. He uses a software 303 for acid lines and sometimes bass, distinguishing those roles. Sample Market interview, 4 February 2024.

Regal's track-by-track account gives contrasting choices: essential percussion with cutoff movement, delayed acid entrances, and evolving combinations of bass, vocals and synths. Regal / ACIDBOY, Attack Magazine, 18 December 2024.

These accounts support several creative processes. They do not justify one fixed entrance order, a mandatory four-chord cycle, one scale, or one narrow tempo interval for all seeds.

3. A useful ensemble for Sottofondo

The following is a proposed palette of musical functions, informed by the evidence above. It is not a claim that all these sounds occur in both references, or a list to enable simultaneously.

FunctionSound to exploreWhat should be composed with it
Acid foregroundArticulated resonant mono voice, from hollow pulse to aggressive distorted sawA recognizable rhythm, rests, accents and selective linked notes; a planned timbral trajectory
Low foundationFirm kick plus optional compact bassComplementary tails and rhythmic placement; decide whether the acid already supplies the bass
Contrasting foregroundSecond acid response, a broad detuned riff, or a short resonant stabAnswer or reinforce the main idea in another register; avoid a competing continuous solo
Sustained depthDark strings, synthetic choir or a filtered droneSlow harmonic support, pedal tones or tension held behind the moving foreground
Rhythmic propulsionShort hats, offbeat open hats, shakers, selected claps/tomsA stable metric pattern; phrase-boundary fills and intentional omissions
Signature and transitionSynthetic vocal-like/formant phrase, metallic effect, reverse swell, delay accentA recurring identifiable event or a specific transition, with space reserved for its tail

For example, a seed with a dense acid foreground might use sparse sustained support. Another might use a simpler acid line below a broad repeated riff. A third might alternate two related acid voices. These are arrangements of roles, not stored songs; their notes, sound parameters and development can still be generated.

Harmonic restraint is a useful experiment for the Blade direction: begin with a tonal centre or sustained support, then introduce chord movement only when it helps the chosen motif. This is a proposed taste constraint, not a claim that acid music forbids changing harmony.

5. Proposed next experiments, in order

First, isolate the acid voice. Preserve one liked seed's pitches, rhythm and accompaniment. Compare current execution with properly connected articulation, then compare a small number of distinct distortion treatments. Keep versions available and use the same output gain. This separates the contribution of articulation from composition changes. A more faithful circuit is useful only if the result improves the music.

Then, compose foreground and support together. Generate several candidate motifs per seed, including their gates and accent/slide gestures. Select using explicit constraints on repetition, contour, rests, tonal fit and kick interaction. Those scores are heuristics; user listening remains the acceptance test. Add the support role that the chosen foreground needs: sustained depth, a second riff, a recurring signature, or a rhythmic response.

Then, expand the development. Generate different entrance orders and phrase-level energy trajectories. Coordinate density, register, filter, envelope, distortion and effect sends. Let the main idea survive enough repetition to become recognizable. Test sections that withhold a sound, transform it and return it; varying bar lengths alone is insufficient.

Keep the process local and versioned. Hash all musical choices by stable domains and musical position. A stateful sounding instrument can still play a deterministically generated score. Seeking must restore or preroll its articulation state; muting must not regenerate decisions. Freeze previous recipes and include the accepted Character seeds 0000001e and 9d2371fe in comparisons.

The immediate deliverable after this research should be a small audible comparison that answers whether the acid foreground has the desired character. The reports cannot promise that an automatic scoring function will reliably choose a hook as memorable as a finished commercial recording. Extending the current 24-patch catalogue before resolving that comparison would add complexity without testing the main hypothesis.

Evidence limits and research record

The evidence is strongest for creator-reported Blade production, documented 303 behavior and the code audit. Colin McRae's detailed arrangement and original studio process remain less certain. No exact BPM, key, chord transcription or hardware list from the model reports has been promoted into a generator rule. The historical interviews are recollections, and the trance workflow sources span different eras and styles.
